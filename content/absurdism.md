- by [[albert camus]]
- "the absurd" is the fact that we are placed on this rock without any instructions
- absurdism is living anyway, without escapism through (1) physical death and (2) [[philosophical suicide]] [e.g. coping, invention of meaning, afterlife, religion]
- death as the end of consciousness
- emphasizes lucid attachment without entitlement (no metaphysical tally of good or bad deeds, no reward after death; not even "i will do x so tomorrow y" — tomorrow is not promised)
- a geologist [[sisyphus]] breaks the argument because the philosophy challenges the absurd. if one happens to like/enjoy what they're doing in this "meaningless life", congrats you just got lucky
